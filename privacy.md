# Privacy Policy — rclone Google Drive sync

This is a personal OAuth client used by one individual to sync files from a
a shared Team Google Drive account to office workstations.

It has no other users. It collects no data. It transmits nothing to any third
party. Credentials are stored locally on a single machine and are never shared.
Data flows only between that machine and Google Drive.
